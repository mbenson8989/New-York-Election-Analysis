# Hamilton County Audit

> **\*2026 figures are unofficial and not certified by the NYS Board of Elections.** They come from the Board's Election Night Reporting page as of 11/21/2026 11:42 AM. The Board says that page leaves out a significant number of unscanned emergency Election Day ballots from Westchester County, and six counties were not at full reporting (see [Sources](Home#sources)). Governor race unless a page says otherwise. Votes are a candidate's votes on every party line he or she appeared on (fusion voting), not a count of registered Democrats who voted. Registration is the Nov 1 enrollment report for each year (active + inactive). These pages measure a pattern; they make no claim about intent, fraud or cause.

## What the audit covered

New York law requires a routine post-election audit in every county. Within fifteen days after each general election, the board of elections or a bipartisan committee must audit the voter verifiable audit records from three percent of voting machines or systems ([NYS BOE election security page](https://elections.ny.gov/election-security); [Election Law § 9-211](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ELN/9-211)).

**No Hamilton County audit records were obtained for this wiki.** The county's 2026 § 9-211 audit sheets are not posted on the NYS BOE sites used here; they would have to be requested from the Hamilton County Board of Elections. So this page audits the **numbers**: every Hamilton figure traced back to the official reports, line by line.

**Why Hamilton is the starting county.** It is the smallest of New York's 62 counties (4,457 registered voters), so every step can be checked by hand. All of its election districts are reported\*, unlike Westchester or Nassau. Its governor-race ballots were the same (2,935) in both years, so a bigger or smaller turnout cannot explain the shift. Registered Democrats fell while Hochul's votes rose, which is the pattern this method looks for. Its ΔG (+5.61\*) ranks 17 of 62 from the bottom, near the county median (+6.68\*), so it is a typical county, not an outlier. The ceiling county, Orange, gained registered Democrats, so it cannot show the residual step; Hamilton can.
